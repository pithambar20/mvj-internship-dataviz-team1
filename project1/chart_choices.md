# Day 3 — Perception and Chart Choice

## Objective
To understand how to choose the correct chart for different data questions and explain why it is suitable.

| No. | Sample Question | Recommended Chart | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How has rainfall changed over the years? | Line Chart | Shows changes and trends over time. |
| 2 | Which month has the highest rainfall? | Bar Chart | Makes it easy to compare rainfall across months. |
| 3 | Which category has the highest value? | Bar Chart | Helps compare values across different categories. |
| 4 | How are rainfall values distributed? | Histogram | Shows the distribution of numerical values. |
| 5 | What percentage does each category contribute to the total? | Pie Chart | Shows parts of a whole when there are only a few categories. |
| 6 | Is there a relationship between rainfall and temperature? | Scatter Plot | Shows the relationship between two numerical variables. |
| 7 | How does rainfall vary by month and year? | Heatmap | Uses colours to reveal patterns across months and years. |
| 8 | Which decade has the highest average rainfall? | Bar Chart | Makes it easy to compare average rainfall across decades. |

## Conclusion
The correct chart depends on the question, the type of data available, and what the reader needs to understand. A good chart should be clear, easy to read, and appropriate for the data.

## Note
Questions involving temperature require temperature data. Analyse them only if the dataset contains the necessary variables.
