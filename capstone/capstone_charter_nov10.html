3 Lakes of Bengaluru — One-Page Capstone Charter Bengaluru

Data: Bengaluru lakes data and reports; BBMP lakes | Source: OpenCity (data.opencity.in)
Task Scope: A lake map of the city with filters, plus a before/after style story page.

Session 25 • Tue 10 Nov
Capstone Kickoff (3 Members)

1. Target Audience

  • Bengaluru Citizens & Lake Action Groups: Residents & neighborhood lake trusts tracking the health, boundaries, and restoration of local ward lakes.
  • Civic Authorities & Planners (BBMP / KTCDA): Officials prioritizing desilting, sewage diversion, and encroachment clearance across zones.
  • Urban Researchers & Journalists: Analysts studying long-term ecological transformation of Bengaluru's water bodies.

2. Team Roles (3 Members)

  • Member 1 (Data & Geospatial Pipeline): Clean OpenCity BBMP lakes dataset, map coordinates/zones, and standardize encroachment & water quality attributes.
  • Member 2 (City Lake Map & Filters): Build View 1 — Interactive Bengaluru map with zone, custodian, area, and health status filters + KPI bar.
  • Member 3 (Before/After Story Page): Build View 2 — Interactive before/after comparison view, restoration timeline, and lake case studies.

3. The 3 Questions the Dashboard Must Answer

Q1. Spatial Distribution & Status Where are Bengaluru's lakes located across BBMP zones/wards, and what is their current condition when filtered by custodian, size, and rejuvenation status?
Q2. Encroachment & Risk Hotspots Which lakes suffer the highest percentage of encroachment, shrinkage, or pollution pressure and require immediate civic intervention?
Q3. Before vs. After Transformation How have key Bengaluru lakes changed before and after urbanization or restoration drives in terms of water spread, wetland recovery, and community impact?
